🔄 How it works

Five steps from install
to live leaderboard

01
Assign KPI roles to your employees
Open any HR Employee record and set their KPI Role to Developer or Implementor. This determines which rule set scores their work.
HR Employee → KPI Role field
02
Configure or use 30+ pre-built rules
Functional managers can adjust point values, add custom rules, or use the pre-built library covering deadlines, complexity, timesheets, bugs, and more — without touching code.
KPI → Rules (Functional Manager access)
03
Work normally in Projects — scores update automatically
Close a task before deadline? Points awarded instantly. Task bounced back to dev? Penalty applied and previous scores cleared. No extra actions needed by the team.
Automation engine runs on task lifecycle events
04
Track progress on the analytics dashboard
Employees see their own rank, points, and charts. Managers drill into any employee or view team aggregates. Weekly charts, doughnut mix, top rules, and recent activity — all real-time.
OWL Dashboard · Team + Personal + Employee views
05
Close periods and review reports
Lock the month, compute final rankings, award gold/silver/bronze badges, then analyse results by employee, period, or rule in Odoo's built-in graph, pivot, and list views.
Reports → By Employee / By Period / By Rule

📈 Dashboard intelligence

A dashboard your team will
actually want to open

Stat cards — 10 KPIs at a glance
Total, positive, negative points; rank with badge; on-time tasks; timesheet compliance %; week vs last week delta; team average comparison.
3 Chart.js visualizations
Stacked bar chart of weekly positive/negative points, a doughnut for points mix, and a horizontal bar for top rule impact — all scoped to the selected period.
Gold / Silver / Bronze leaderboard
Top 10 performers with avatars, rank badges, and total/positive/negative breakdown. Managers see the full team; employees see their position in context.
Three view modes
Personal — your own stats and rank. Team — aggregated overview. Employee — drill into any team member via avatar picker.
Period navigation & state badges
Browse up to 14 months back with prev/next arrows or the dropdown. Period state (Draft / Open / Closed) is always visible. One click to refresh.
